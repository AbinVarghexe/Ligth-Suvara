ABSTRACT
In an effort to empower individuals with color blindness, this project proposes a web application
that bridges the divide in color perception. The application utilizes real-time camera analysis,
employing a central cursor to pinpoint specific colors within the user’s field of view. Functionality
extends beyond live camera use, allowing users to upload images and identify colors at designated
points within them. This surpasses simple color naming by offering user-friendly descriptions
that bridge the gap through relatable comparisons or real-world applications of the identified
colors. To further enrich the user experience, the application generates complementary and
similar color palettes based on the detected color. This comprehensive approach fosters a
deeper understanding of color for users with color blindness, ultimately enhancing their ability
to navigate and interact with the visual world in a more confident and meaningful way.

INTRODUCTION
1.1 Overview
This web application aids color-blind individuals in identifying colors. It uses the device
camera for real-time color detection with a central cursor and allows image uploads for color
analysis. The application provides descriptions of colors, their real-life applications, and lists of
contrasting and similar colors.
1.2 Objective
According to the latest studies,it is estimated that around 300 million people worldwide have
color blindness.In men, the prevalence of color blindness is around 5.0% to 8.0%, while in
women the prevalence is only 0.5% to 1.0%. The lack of efficient applications that aid the
day-to-day activities of a colorblind person in the market pays to the significance of the web
application,’Bloom’. The primary objective of this web application is to empower color-blind
individuals by providing them with a reliable tool for color detection and understanding. By
utilizing the device camera for live color identification and enabling image uploads for analysis,
the application aims to offer a comprehensive solution for recognizing and interpreting colors in
everyday life. The descriptions and color lists are intended to enrich the user’s knowledge and
assist in making informed decisions related to color. [1]

cation designed specifically for colorblind individuals. The application aims to assist colorblind

click on a specific point within the image to identify the color at that location. This combined
functionality enables users to determine colors either directly from a live camera feed or from
images stored on their device.

Privacy Policy

Light Suvara - Sunday School App

Last Updated: November 6, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Light Suvara. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Light Suvara is a Sunday School management application serving Christian Mission League (CML) and SUVARA organizations. We take your privacy seriously and are transparent about our data practices.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

When you register and use Light Suvara, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number
  • Profile Information: Organization affiliation (CML or SUVARA), role (User or Admin)
  • School Selection: Your selected Sunday School organization

2.2 Usage Information

We automatically collect certain information when you use our app:

  • Event Interactions: Events you view, search queries, event registrations
  • App Usage: Features accessed, time spent in app, navigation patterns
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers

2.3 Content You Create

If you are an Admin user, we collect:

  • Event Data: Event titles, descriptions, dates, venues, images
  • Notifications: Broadcast messages and announcements
  • Media Files: Images and documents uploaded for events

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide and maintain Light Suvara functionality
  • Personalization: To filter and display relevant events based on your organization
  • Communications: To send you event notifications, updates, and important announcements
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with our app and improve user experience
  • Security: To protect against unauthorized access and ensure data integrity
  • Admin Functions: To enable event management, reporting, and administrative tasks

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Firebase Infrastructure

Light Suvara uses Google Firebase services for secure data storage and management:

  • Firebase Authentication: Secure user authentication and authorization
  • Cloud Firestore: Real-time database for events, users, and notifications
  • Firebase Storage: Secure cloud storage for images and documents
  • Cloud Messaging: Push notification delivery system

4.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Data encryption in transit and at rest
  • Role-based access control (User vs. Admin permissions)
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Secure Firebase Security Rules to prevent unauthorized access

Note: While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 Within Your Organization

Event information is shared within your selected organization (CML or SUVARA) to facilitate Sunday School activities and communication.

5.2 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: Backend infrastructure and data storage
  • Google Fonts: Typography and UI enhancement (no personal data shared)

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5.4 What We DON'T Do

We explicitly do NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Use your information for marketing purposes outside our app
  • Track you across other apps or websites

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Access and Update

You have the right to access and update your personal information through the Profile section of the app.

6.2 Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at support@lightsuvara.com. We will delete your data within 30 days of your request.

6.3 Notification Preferences

You can manage your notification preferences through your device settings or within the app.

6.4 Organization Change

You can change your Sunday School organization selection at any time through the School Selection screen.

7. Children's Privacy

Light Suvara is intended for use by Sunday School participants and administrators. While we may serve users of various ages, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Specific retention periods:

  • User Accounts: Retained until account deletion is requested
  • Event Data: Retained indefinitely for historical records unless deletion is requested
  • Notifications: Retained for 90 days
  • Usage Logs: Retained for 12 months for analytics purposes

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and maintained on servers located outside of your country of residence. By using Light Suvara, you consent to the transfer of information to countries outside your country of residence, which may have different data protection rules.

We ensure that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws and that your data receives adequate protection.

10. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Light Suvara is a native mobile application and does not use cookies. However, we do use:

  • Firebase Analytics: To understand app usage patterns
  • Local Storage: To cache data and improve app performance
  • Device Identifiers: To enable push notifications and maintain sessions

You can disable analytics through your device settings, though this may limit some app functionality.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. When we make changes:

  • We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • We will notify you through the app or via email for material changes
  • Your continued use of Light Suvara after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

12. Third-Party Links

Light Suvara may contain links to external websites or resources (such as Bible study materials or event registration pages). This Privacy Policy applies only to our app. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.
